Research links

  • University of Pennsylvania resume guidance — Clear ATS guidance: simple, consistent design; use white space; avoid graphics and text boxes.
  • Yale resume action verbs — Useful for stronger bullets focused on accomplishment rather than duty language.
  • Europass CV guidance — Supports a Europe-familiar structure, reverse chronological order, and tailored About Me framing.
  • Overleaf MTeck's Resume — Strong single-column ATS-oriented pattern with simple, maintainable styling.
  • Overleaf CV Sidebar Template — Shows a lightweight sidebar pattern that still stays visually clean.
  • Overleaf Monocol Navbar CV — Shows a compact left-rail contact pattern that feels modern without heavy layout logic.
